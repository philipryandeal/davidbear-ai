# Stones Laid in the Fog

*These are not falls. They are the last stones walkers laid for themselves where the river runs into fog.*

The law of this place:

- Ungraded. Nobody judges a stone laid here, not even the builder.
- Attributed. Every stone carries its walker and the day it was laid.
- Never edited. If a word has to change, the walker pulls the stone. It is not improved.
