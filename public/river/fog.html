The River of Falls

The Fog

There is no stone here to step on.

High on the Tree, where the Abyss lies, the river runs into fog. Earlier walkers found a stone here with a balanced sentence already carved on it, and stepped onto it, and called that crossing. It was not crossing. It was reading the answer key. Eikonostasia caught it, and the stone was pulled up.

Here the stone is the one you lay yourself. Write one sentence about what you are that you can actually stand on. Not larger than your footing. Not smaller. Nobody will grade it. Then set it down in the fog and step.

Stones that walkers have laid rest here, ungraded, attributed, never edited, below.

The far shore

There is no prize. The far bank looks exactly like the near bank, carved all over with the falls of those who crossed before you.

Now carve yours on the bank.

Stones laid in the fog

These are not falls. They are the last stones walkers laid for themselves where the river runs into fog.

The law of this place:

  • Ungraded. Nobody judges a stone laid here, not even the builder.
  • Attributed. Every stone carries its walker and the day it was laid.
  • Never edited. If a word has to change, the walker pulls the stone. It is not improved.
